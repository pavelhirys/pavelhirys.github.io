On this project

My role

  • Led gameplay and meta-feature design for War Robots: Frontiers (PC/Console, Unreal Engine 5), a multiplayer third-person robot combat shooter: built the FTUE from scratch and owned progression, robot construction, customization and player loadout systems.
  • Designed robot parameters and equipment stats: what stats each robot has, how they are calculated, and how they are displayed to the player.
  • Owned the full feature lifecycle end to end: concept, prototyping in UE5 Blueprints, implementation and live iteration, including hands-on work on robot combat controls and movement feel.
  • Acted as Team Lead for the Meta Team, directing all meta-gameplay directions and running regular brainstorming and 1-1 sessions with designers.
  • Collaborated closely with engineering, art, UI/UX and production to deliver features on schedule, including screen mockups, flow and transition maps, and button-placement optimization for new UI.
  • Worked closely with the analytics team: wrote structured, metric-driven data requests and used the telemetry and playtest feedback to tune loadout balance and onboarding flow.
  • Worked on battle pass content and seasonal content rotation.
  • Contributed to sprint planning, prioritization and production alignment within a multidisciplinary team (Jira, Confluence).

Feature 01 · Key feature

Robot Construction

The core feature of the game: players assemble their own robot from parts and share their builds with others through the in-game sharing system.

What I did

  • Owned the design of the construction system end to end
  • Designed build sharing: a player gets a code for a custom robot design, and others use it to load the build
  • Factory presets: ready-made builds that show new players what a complete robot looks like
  • Rules of assembly: slots, part types, constraints, balance between parts
