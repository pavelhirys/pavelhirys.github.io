Feature 01 · Key feature

Robot Construction

The core feature of the game: players assemble their own robot from parts and share their builds with others through the in-game sharing system.

Goal

Why the feature mattered: what player need it answered, what the team wanted from it (self-expression, depth, long-term retention).

What I did

  • Owned the design of the construction system end to end
  • Designed build sharing: how players publish a robot and how others find and use it
  • Rules of assembly: slots, part types, constraints, balance between parts
  • UX of the builder: how a new player makes a first robot without getting lost

Result

Metric or outcome: share of players who built a custom robot, number of shared builds, feedback from playtests.
